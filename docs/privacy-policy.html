Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Tech Hotspot Daily ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our site.

Information We Collect

Google Analytics 4 (GA4)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors interact with our site. GA4 collects anonymized data including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Browser type and device information
  • General geographic location (country/city level)
  • Referring website or traffic source

We have enabled IP anonymization (anonymize_ip: true) so your full IP address is never stored or logged. GA4 data is used solely to improve our content and user experience.

Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display contextual advertisements. AdSense may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site or other websites. You can learn more about Google's advertising practices at Google's Advertising Policy.

Newsletter Subscription

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. We use this solely to send you daily tech news digests. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email. We do not share your email with third parties.

Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right to access — Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure — Request deletion of your data.
  • Right to restrict processing — Limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability — Receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — Object to our processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

Cookies

Our site uses a minimal, privacy-conscious cookie approach:

  • Consent choice — We store your cookie preference in your browser's localStorage (no server-side storage). This is not a cookie but a local preference flag.
  • Google Analytics — Uses cookies to distinguish unique visitors and track session information. These are only loaded after you explicitly accept cookies.
  • Google AdSense — Uses cookies for personalized advertising. These are only loaded after you explicitly accept cookies.

No scripts are loaded until you click "Accept" on the cookie consent banner. You may decline and still access all content on the site.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Data is shared only with:

  • Google — For analytics (GA4) and advertising (AdSense), subject to Google's privacy policy.
  • Resend — For sending newsletter emails, subject to Resend's privacy policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
